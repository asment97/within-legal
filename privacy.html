隐私政策

关于 WITHIN 如何处理你和 Ta 的资料

简单说： WITHIN 是一个为两个人设计的私密空间。我们只收集让 app 正常运作所需的资料；不分析、不卖出、不与第三方共享。你随时可以删除账户，所有资料连同 30 天的缓冲期一起被清除。

1. 这个 app 是谁做的

WITHIN 是一位独立开发者制作的个人项目，不属于任何注册公司。所有问题或请求都可以发到 vibestack.tan@gmail.com。

2. 我们收集什么资料

2.1 注册账号时

  • 电邮地址（来自你选择的 Google 登录）—— 用于身份认证和与 Ta 的连接。
  • 显示名称、头像（你在「个人资料」中自己填写）。

2.2 使用 app 时

  • 你输入的内容：小事、心愿、目标、纪念日、心情记录、留言。这些只属于你们两个人。
  • 你上传的照片（仅当你主动选择上传到「回忆」时），加密存储。
  • 粗略位置（仅在你授权时）—— 用于显示当地天气，精确到城市级别，不存储完整经纬度。
  • 设备推送 token（Firebase Cloud Messaging）—— 用于让 Ta 的小提醒到达你。

2.3 我们不收集

  • 你的通讯录、相册（除了你主动选择的那一张）、其他 app 的使用记录。
  • 广告 ID、设备指纹、行为追踪。
  • 完整 GPS 坐标、出行轨迹。

3. 资料如何被保护

  • 所有数据通过 HTTPS / TLS 1.2+ 加密传输。
  • 后端存储（Supabase / PostgreSQL）启用 Row-Level Security —— 你只能读到属于你们这对的资料，连数据库管理员也看不到他人的内容。
  • 照片以 Supabase Storage 加密存放，访问通过短时有效的签名链接。
  • 密码由 Supabase Auth 用 bcrypt 散列存储（如果你将来选择电邮密码登录）。

4. 谁能看到这些资料

  • 你 —— 通过你的账号。
  • Ta（你绑定的伴侣） —— WITHIN 的核心就是「两个人共享」，所以小事、心愿、心情记录默认对 Ta 可见。每一处都可以在创建时关掉「同步给 Ta」。
  • WITHIN 开发者 —— 只在你主动通过支持邮箱要求帮助时，并经你同意之后。
  • 第三方 —— 不会。我们不把你的资料卖给任何人，也不用作广告或分析（除了 Firebase Crashlytics 收集匿名的崩溃报告 —— 不含任何你的内容）。

5. 资料存放在哪里

资料存放在 Supabase 的服务器上（区域：新加坡 / Asia-Southeast）。Firebase 服务（崩溃报告、推送）由 Google 在区域内的数据中心运行。

6. 资料留存时间

  • 账号活跃期间，资料一直保留以确保 app 正常使用。
  • 当你触发「删除账号」时（设置 → 账号 → 删除我的账号），账号立即被标记为待删除，进入 30 天宽容期。
  • 宽容期内，你可以通过重新登录 app 取消删除。
  • 30 天后，所有资料（包括 Ta 与你共享的部分）被永久清除。删除账号同时会让你脱离与 Ta 的连接 —— 这是目前唯一的「解绑」方式。
  • 崩溃报告日志最多保留 90 天，由 Firebase Crashlytics 自动循环。

7. 你的权利

根据马来西亚《个人资料保护法 2010》（PDPA），你有权：

  • 访问你的资料 —— 设置 → 账号 → 导出我的资料（JSON 格式）。
  • 更正你的资料 —— 直接在 app 中编辑个人资料、心情、小事等。
  • 删除你的资料 —— 设置 → 账号 → 删除我的账号，或访问 网页版删除入口。
  • 撤回同意 —— 关掉推送、位置等系统权限，或直接删除账号。

8. 儿童保护

WITHIN 是面向 17 岁及以上的用户设计的（App Store / Play 评级：Mature 17+）。我们不刻意收集 17 岁以下用户的资料；如果发现，资料会被立即删除。

9. 政策的更新

这份政策可能因为功能调整或法规要求而更新。重大改动会在 app 内推送通知，并把新版本发布在 privacy.html。生效日期在文首。

10. 联系我们

关于隐私的任何问题、请求或投诉，请发邮件到 vibestack.tan@gmail.com。我们会在合理时间内（通常 7 个工作日内）回复。

Privacy policy

How WITHIN handles your and your partner's data

The short version. WITHIN is a private space for two people. We collect only what's needed for the app to work — no analytics resale, no third-party sharing. You can delete your account any time and everything is wiped after a 30-day grace period.

1. Who makes this app

WITHIN is a personal project built by an independent developer; it is not operated by a registered company. Any questions or requests can be sent to vibestack.tan@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

2.1 At sign-up

  • Email address (from the Google sign-in you choose) — used for authentication and to connect you with your partner.
  • Display name and avatar — only what you provide in your profile.

2.2 While you use the app

  • Content you create: little things, wishes, goals, anniversaries, mood check-ins, notes. These are visible only to the two of you.
  • Photos you upload (only those you actively pick for memories), stored encrypted.
  • Coarse location (only with your permission) — used to show local weather at city granularity. Full latitude/longitude is never stored.
  • Device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — so quiet reminders from your partner can reach you.

2.3 What we do NOT collect

  • Your contacts, photo library (except the picture you choose), other apps' usage records.
  • Advertising IDs, device fingerprints, behavioural tracking.
  • Precise GPS coordinates or movement traces.

3. How data is protected

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS / TLS 1.2+.
  • The backend (Supabase / PostgreSQL) uses Row-Level Security — you can only read rows that belong to your couple; even database operators cannot see other couples' content.
  • Photos are stored encrypted in Supabase Storage; access uses short-lived signed URLs.
  • Passwords (if you switch to email/password sign-in in the future) are bcrypt-hashed by Supabase Auth.

4. Who can see your data

  • You, through your account.
  • Your partner (the person you bind with). WITHIN is built on the premise of sharing between two people, so little things, wishes, mood entries are visible to them by default. Anything sensitive can be marked "私密 / private" at the moment you create it.
  • The WITHIN developer, only when you actively request support and consent to share details.
  • Third parties: no. We do not sell your data, run ads, or share with analytics partners — apart from Firebase Crashlytics, which receives anonymous crash reports that contain no content of yours.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored on Supabase servers (region: Singapore / Asia-Southeast). Firebase services (crash reports, push) are operated by Google in the same regional cluster.

6. How long we keep it

  • While your account is active, your data is retained so the app works.
  • When you trigger "Delete my account" (Settings → Account → Delete my account), the account is immediately marked for deletion and a 30-day grace period begins.
  • During the grace period, signing back into the app cancels the deletion.
  • After 30 days, everything (including content shared with your partner) is permanently purged. Deleting the account also detaches you from your partner — that is currently the only "unbind" mechanism.
  • Crash report logs are retained for up to 90 days by Firebase Crashlytics on its standard rolling window.

7. Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), you may:

  • Access your data — Settings → Account → Export my data (JSON).
  • Correct your data — edit your profile, moods, or little things directly in the app.
  • Delete your data — Settings → Account → Delete my account, or use the web delete portal.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off notification or location permissions in your OS settings, or delete the account.

8. Children

WITHIN is designed for users aged 17 and above (App Store / Play rating: Mature 17+). We do not knowingly collect data from users under 17; if discovered, it is deleted promptly.

9. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated for new features or regulatory changes. Significant changes are announced in the app and the new version is published at privacy.html. The effective date is shown at the top.

10. Contact

For any privacy questions, requests, or complaints, email vibestack.tan@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 business days.
