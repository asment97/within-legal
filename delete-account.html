删除我的 WITHIN 账户

这个入口让你在不打开 app 的情况下删除账户。流程和 app 内的「设置 → 账号 → 删除我的账号」完全相同。

会发生什么？
  • 你的账户会立即被标记为待删除，并从你和 Ta 的连接中移出。
  • 接下来的 30 天是宽容期 —— 你可以重新登录 app 取消删除。
  • 30 天后，所有资料（包括与 Ta 共享的部分）会被永久清除。
  • 这是目前唯一的「解绑」方式 —— 删除账户会同时把你和 Ta 的连接断开。

请输入你登录 WITHIN 时使用的电邮地址。我们会发一条「魔法链接」到那个邮箱，点击它即可继续。

Delete my WITHIN account

This portal lets you delete your account without opening the app. The flow is identical to the in-app one (Settings → Account → Delete my account).

What happens:
  • Your account is immediately marked for deletion and detached from the couple connection.
  • A 30-day grace period follows — signing back in to the app cancels the deletion.
  • After 30 days, all data (including content shared with your partner) is permanently purged.
  • This is currently the only "unbind" mechanism — deleting the account also detaches you from your partner.

Enter the email address you signed into WITHIN with. We'll send a one-time "magic link" to that inbox; click it to continue.

✉ 邮件已发出。请到收件箱查找「WITHIN 删除账户」的链接，点击后会回到这里。链接 60 分钟内有效。

✉ Email sent. Look for "WITHIN delete account" in your inbox and click the link — it brings you back here. The link is valid for 60 minutes.
