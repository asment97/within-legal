常见问题

1. 怎么和 Ta 绑定？

登录后，在「连接」页面会有两个选择：

  • 创建邀请码：生成一个 6 位数邀请码或二维码，把它给 Ta。
  • 输入对方的邀请码：扫码或输入数字。

绑定一次即可，之后所有内容会自动同步。

2. 绑定后能换 Ta 吗？

v1 版本不支持「解绑」—— 删除账户是目前唯一的退出方式。这是有意为之，详见《使用条款》第 4 条。未来版本会加入更柔和的「软解绑」流程。

3. 怎么删除账户？

  • 在 app 内：设置 → 账号 → 删除我的账号
  • 在网页上：delete-account.html

两种方式效果相同：账户进入 30 天宽容期，期间重新登录可以撤销。

4. 怎么导出我的资料？

在 app 内：设置 → 账号 → 导出我的资料。系统会生成一份 JSON 文件，包含你所有的小事、心愿、纪念日、心情记录、个人资料等。

5. 推送通知收不到？

  • 检查系统通知权限（设置 → app → WITHIN → 通知）。
  • 检查 app 内：设置 → 通知 → 各项开关是否开启。
  • 「安静时段」默认是 22:00 – 08:00，这段时间内的推送会延后到次日早上。

6. 看到 bug 了？

发邮件到 vibestack.tan@gmail.com，附上：

  • 设备 + 系统版本（例如「Pixel 8 / Android 15」）。
  • app 版本（设置 → 关于）。
  • 能复现 bug 的具体步骤。
  • 截图（如果方便的话）。

7. WITHIN Plus 什么时候上线？

v1 是 Android 先行版本，所有功能完全免费。WITHIN Plus 订阅会在 v1.1 上线（同时加入 iOS 版本）。在那之前，所有标着「即将上线」的功能都不会扣费。

8. 隐私和数据安全

详见 隐私政策。要点：

  • 资料只在你和 Ta 之间共享，不卖给任何人，不用作广告。
  • 后端 Row-Level Security 确保你只能看到属于你们这对的资料。
  • 位置精确到城市级别，不存储完整 GPS 坐标。

Frequently asked

1. How do I bind with my partner?

After signing in, the Bind screen offers two options:

  • Create an invite code: generates a 6-digit code + QR — share either with your partner.
  • Enter your partner's code: scan the QR or type the 6 digits.

You only bind once — afterwards everything syncs automatically.

2. Can I switch partners after binding?

v1 does not support "unbind" — deleting the account is the only way out. That's deliberate; see Terms §4. A gentler "soft unbind" flow is planned for a future release.

3. How do I delete my account?

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account
  • On the web: delete-account.html

Both paths behave identically: the account enters a 30-day grace period; signing back in during that window cancels the deletion.

4. How do I export my data?

In the app: Settings → Account → Export my data. You'll receive a JSON file containing all your little things, wishes, anniversaries, mood entries, profile, and so on.

5. Notifications aren't arriving?

  • Check OS-level permission: Settings → apps → WITHIN → Notifications.
  • Check in-app: Settings → Notifications — confirm the per-type switches are on.
  • "Quiet hours" defaults to 22:00 – 08:00; pushes during that window are queued and delivered next morning.

6. Found a bug?

Email vibestack.tan@gmail.com with:

  • Device + OS version (e.g. "Pixel 8 / Android 15").
  • App version (Settings → About).
  • Specific steps to reproduce.
  • A screenshot if convenient.

7. When does WITHIN Plus launch?

v1 is Android-first; everything is free. The WITHIN Plus subscription ships in v1.1 alongside the iOS release. Until then, any feature marked "即将上线 / coming soon" stays free.

8. Privacy & data

See the privacy policy. Highlights:

  • Data is shared only between you and your partner. Never sold, never used for ads.
  • Row-Level Security on the backend ensures you can only read rows belonging to your couple.
  • Location is coarse (city granularity); no precise GPS coordinates are stored.
