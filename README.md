# WITHIN

A quiet, private space for two.

WITHIN is a small mobile app for couples — a shared place to keep the
little things, wishes, anniversaries, mood check-ins, and notes that
belong to just the two of you. No feeds, no followers, no ads. Just
the two of you, in one place.

> 一个为两个人设计的私密空间 —— 一起记下小事、心愿、纪念日、心情和留言。没有信息流，没有粉丝，没有广告。只属于你们两个人。

## Quick answers

- **Who makes WITHIN?** An independent developer — not a company. See
  the [privacy policy](privacy.html) for the full disclosure.
- **Where is my data stored?** Supabase, Singapore region. Photos are
  encrypted at rest; everything moves over HTTPS. Details in
  [§3 and §5 of the privacy policy](privacy.html).
- **How do I delete my account?** Either from the app
  (Settings → Account → Delete my account) or from
  [the web portal](delete-account.html). Both start a 30-day grace
  period — sign back in during that window to cancel.
- **How do I export my data?** In the app: Settings → Account → Export
  my data. You get a JSON file with all your content.
- **Found a bug or have a question?** Email
  [vibestack.tan@gmail.com](mailto:vibestack.tan@gmail.com). Replies
  typically arrive within 7 business days.
